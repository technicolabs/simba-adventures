Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to visit Tanzania? +
The dry season from June to October offers the best game viewing, including the Great Migration river crossings. The calving season in January–February is also spectacular.
How far in advance should I book? +
We recommend booking 6–12 months ahead for peak season (July–September) to secure the best camps. Off-peak trips can often be arranged with 2–3 months' notice.
Are your safaris suitable for families? +
Absolutely. We design family-friendly itineraries with shorter drive times, family tents, and guides experienced in engaging younger travellers.
What is included in a safari with Simba Adventures? +
Our safari packages generally include a 4x4 Safari Land Cruiser with a pop-up roof, a professional English-speaking guide, fuel, vehicle and park fees, national park and conservation fees, accommodation and meals according to your itinerary, drinking water, airport transfers from JRO or Arusha, and AMREF / Flying Doctors emergency evacuation cover. Exact inclusions can vary by itinerary and will be confirmed before booking.
What isn't included in the safari price? +
International flights, travel insurance, Tanzania visa fees, guide tips, personal expenses, alcoholic drinks and soft drinks are not included. Optional experiences such as balloon safaris, Maasai village visits, night game drives and walking safaris are also extra unless specifically included in your itinerary. Accommodation before or after your safari in Arusha can be arranged separately.

Privacy Policy

At Simba Adventures, we take your personal privacy seriously. This document outlines how we collect, handle, and secure your personal details during safari bookings.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential personal information required to process national park permits, accommodations, and internal flights, including full names, passport details, phone numbers, and dietary preferences.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is strictly used for booking safari lodges, processing government conservation permits, and organizing local concierge transportation across East Africa.

3. Data Protection & Sharing

We never sell or distribute your personal details to third-party advertisers. Information is only shared with accredited park authorities and partner lodges essential to your itinerary.

Our Story

Tanzania is our home

Jambo! Welcome to Simba Adventures.

We're a locally owned safari company based in Arusha, the heart of Tanzania's safari country. With nearly 20 years on the ground, we know these lands inside and out — the best routes, the hidden spots, and the right timing for every adventure.

From wildlife safaris and Kilimanjaro climbs to cultural experiences, we plan trips around what matters to you — not a fixed package.

Our goal is simple: make your African trip feel easy and personal, from the first message to the day you fly home.

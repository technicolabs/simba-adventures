Our Story

Tanzania is our home

Jambo! Welcome to Simba Adventures.

We're a locally owned safari company based in Arusha, the heart of Tanzania's safari country. We've been guiding here for 20 years, so we know these lands inside and out — the best routes, the hidden spots, and the right timing for every adventure.

From wildlife safaris and Kilimanjaro climbs to cultural experiences, we plan trips around what matters to you — not a fixed package.

Our goal is simple: make your African trip feel easy and personal, from the first message to the day you fly home.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Responsible Travel

We believe travel should do right by the places it touches. That means responsible wildlife viewing, working with local camps and guides, and not pushing you into more than you asked for.

Personalized Service

There's no "one size fits all" here. We listen first, then shape a trip around how you like to travel — your pace, your interests, your budget.

Wildlife & Nature

From the Serengeti to Ngorongoro and beyond, wildlife comes first. We keep our distance, follow the rules of the parks, and never crowd an animal for the sake of a photo.

Expert Guidance

Our guides are locals who've grown up in these landscapes. They'll answer your questions straight — from animal behaviour to what's really worth seeing in a day.

Meet The Team

The People Behind Your Adventure

AS

Ally Simba

Founder & Head Guide

Ally started Simba Adventures back in 2006 after years of guiding himself. He still leads trips fairly often — says it keeps him honest about what guests actually need out here.

SM

Saidi Mange

Safari Operations Director

Saidi runs the day-to-day: vehicles, camps, park permits, timing. If a trip runs smoothly, it's because of the quiet work he does before you even land. Nothing here happens without his checklist.

BM

Brenda Moshi

Guest Experience Manager

Brenda is the one answering your emails and making sure nothing slips through the cracks — dietary notes, airport pickups, last-minute changes. Guests usually mention her by name when they write back to us.

SJ

Our Story

Tanzania is our home

Welcome to Simba Adventures, a premier, locally owned tour and safari agency based in Africa, the heart of safari country— Arusha, Tanzania.

We specialize in creating unforgettable journeys to Tanzania, offering a range of safari tours, Kilimanjaro climbs, and cultural experiences.

Our mission is to turn your dream African adventure into reality with personalized service and expert guidance.

Whether you're looking to explore the wildlife, conquer the highest peak in Africa, or immerse yourself in local culture, we are here to make it happen.

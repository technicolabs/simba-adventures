Our Story

Tanzania is our home territory

Simba Adventures was founded in 2006 by Josephat Mrema, a third-generation Tanzanian whose grandfather was among the first rangers in the Serengeti. What started as a small guiding operation out of Arusha has grown to over 60 guides and support staff — all Tanzanian, all passionate.

We have guided more than 3,400 guests from 62 countries through Tanzania's most spectacular corridors. From first-time safari-goers to seasoned wildlife photographers, each guest receives the same meticulous care and personalised attention that Josephat poured into that very first trip.

Today, Simba Adventures holds full KATO certification, Eco-Tourism accreditation, and is a proud member of the African Travel & Tourism Association. But our greatest pride remains the stories our guests carry home.

What We Stand For

Our Values

Conservation First

We partner exclusively with eco-certified camps. A percentage of every booking funds anti-poaching and community development programs in Tanzania.

Community Impact

Our guides, drivers, and lodge staff are Tanzanian. We actively invest in local schools, women-led cooperatives, and Maasai cultural preservation.

Wildlife Integrity

We never influence animal behaviour for a photo. No off-road driving in sensitive areas — the animals' welfare always comes before the perfect shot.

Honest Excellence

We tell you the truth about what you'll see, what it costs, and how to prepare. No upsells, no hidden fees — just exceptional safaris at clear prices.

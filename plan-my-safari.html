Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the best time to visit Tanzania?+
June to October is prime dry season for game viewing; January–February brings the calving season on the southern plains.
How far in advance should I book?+
6–12 months ahead for peak season camps; 2–3 months is usually fine off-peak.
Are your safaris suitable for families with children?+
Yes — we tailor pacing, accommodation, and activities for travellers of all ages.
Is Tanzania safe for travellers?+
Yes. Tanzania is one of East Africa's most stable destinations for tourism, and our guides and vehicles are with you throughout your trip.
What is included in the safari package? +
A 4x4 Safari Land Cruiser with pop-up roof, a professional English-speaking guide, fuel, driver fees and vehicle park entry fees. All National Park entry fees, crater fees, concession fees, government taxes and the Ngorongoro Crater service fee. Accommodation as per the itinerary (lodge or tented camp), all meals on safari, lunch boxes and drinking water (1.5L per person per day). Airport pickup and drop-off from JRO or Arusha, first aid kit, binoculars, and AMREF / Flying Doctors emergency evacuation cover.
What is not included? +
International flights to and from Tanzania, travel insurance (must cover medical and evacuation), Tanzania visa ($50–$100 on arrival), tips or gratuities ($15 per day for the guide), personal items like laundry, phone bills, alcoholic drinks and sodas, and extras such as balloon safari, Maasai village visit, night game drive or walking safari unless stated. Accommodation before and after the safari in Arusha can be arranged on request.

Why Simba

Tanzania is our home territory

Simba Adventures started in Arusha in 2006 and has since guided over 3,400 guests into Tanzania's wild places. We're Tanzanians who grew up here — we know these lands, the wildlife, and the right times to go.

100% Locally Owned & Operated

The company is staffed and owned by native Tanzanians, and your guides have a lifelong knowledge of wildlife tracking, regional ecosystems, and local culture.

Attentive Custom Vehicles

Custom-designed 4x4 Land Cruisers with pop-up photographic hatches, extended dust-proofing, built-in coolers, and complimentary binoculars.

Fair Prices, Straight Quotes

Best price guaranteed approach. Our packages span budget-conscious to luxury, letting you match your comfort level to your budget.

A Track Record We're Proud Of

Perfect 5-star reviews on TripAdvisor and highly rated feedback on SafariBookings and Google — built on how we handle travellers, not luck.

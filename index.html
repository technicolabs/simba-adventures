Why Simba

Tanzania is our
home territory

Simba Adventures started in Arusha in 2006 and has since guided over 3,400 guests into Tanzania's wild places. We're Tanzanians who grew up here — we know these lands, the wildlife, and the right times to go.

🌍

100% Locally Owned & Operated

The company is staffed and owned by native Tanzanians, your guides have an intimate, lifelong knowledge of wildlife tracking, regional ecosystems, and local culture.

🚙

Highly Attentive Custom Vehicles

Custom-designed 4x4 Toyota Land Cruisers equipped with pop-up photographic roof hatches, extended dust-proofing, built-in coolers with complimentary binoculars.

💰

Excellent Price-to-Quality Ratio

Best price guaranteed approach. Our packages span from budget-conscious to mid-range and luxury tours, letting you scale your comfort level precisely to your budget.

⭐

Stellar Track Record

Perfect 5-star reviews on TripAdvisor and highly rated feedback on platforms like SafariBookings and Google with travelers for our exceptional handling.
